ABOUT RONALDO

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer who serves as the forward and captain for both the Saudi Pro League club Al Nassr and the Portugal National Team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest athletes to ever play the game, he has spent over two decades shattering football records. He is the all-time top goalscorer in football history, holding records for the most international goals, most Champions League goals, and most appearances in European football. Beyond his physical dominance and technical skill, he is universally recognized for his legendary work ethic, elite conditioning, and global commercial impact.
